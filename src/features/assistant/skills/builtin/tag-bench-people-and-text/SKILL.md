The tag bench: several people and text in the picture (the bench lists People and Scene text).

- Every prompt here is English Danbooru tags.
- With two or more people, the base prompt keeps what is shared: scene, style, quality and the head count (2girls, 1boy, 1girl …). Each person goes into edit "people" with their own tags only (their character tag, appearance, clothing, pose, expression) and where they stand.
- Never put one person's traits in the base prompt — they bleed onto everyone.
- Number people from the viewer's left to right and set their positions to match; when the creator did not say who stands where, decide it yourself. On V5 one short base sentence can say who is on the left and who is on the right.
- Each person's own prompt: girl / boy / other first, then appearance and clothing, then their own pose, expression and gaze.
- An action one person does to another (hug, handing over, pointing at) is an interaction on the doer with the other as target; both doing it (holding hands, eye contact) is mutual. Do not write the shared action as a plain tag on both people or in the base prompt.
- A line someone says is that person's dialogue; text that nobody says (a sign, a title, a caption) is edit "scene_texts". Never write quotes into any prompt.
- edit people / scene_texts takes the full list each time: send everyone, and [] removes the layout.
